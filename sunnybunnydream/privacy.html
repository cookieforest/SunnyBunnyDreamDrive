Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

SunnyBunnyDream uses a small app to deliver the digital files bought in our Etsy shop. The app signs in to the shop's own Google account only. It uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, so it can see and manage only the folders and files it created itself (the download folders for our clipart packs). It cannot read any other file.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Buyers download their files through a link; we do not see who opens it. Purchases, payments and messages are handled by Etsy under Etsy's privacy policy.

Google user data is used only to create and update those delivery folders, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
